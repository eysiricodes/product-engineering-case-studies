# Flurzi Commit

## Product

Flurzi Commit is a UK fintech mobile product centred on credit planning and stronger financial habits.

## Role

Full Stack Developer.

## Responsibilities

- Delivered product work across mobile and backend-connected experiences.
- Built user-facing flows with an emphasis on clear, practical interaction.
- Supported API integration, authentication, release preparation, and app-store delivery.
- Worked with product requirements through implementation, testing, and iteration.

## Engineering Considerations

Financial products need users to understand what they are doing at each step. The engineering focus was therefore on reliable flows, readable information, predictable states, and a release process suitable for a live mobile product.

## Confidentiality Note

This work was completed for a client startup. Technical implementation, product metrics, and proprietary business information remain private.
