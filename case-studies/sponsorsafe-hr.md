# SponsorSafe HR

## Product

SponsorSafe HR is a UK-focused sponsor-licence compliance platform supporting right-to-work record management, visa reminders, and audit-evidence workflows.

## Role

Full Stack Developer.

## Responsibilities

- Built product functionality across responsive web interfaces and backend-connected workflows.
- Worked on structured records, reminders, and evidence-oriented user journeys.
- Applied accessibility and responsive-design considerations so operational tasks could be completed clearly across devices.
- Supported iterative delivery in a product area where accuracy, traceability, and user confidence matter.

## Engineering Considerations

Compliance products must make complex administrative work easier without obscuring important information. The work focused on clear user flows, dependable data presentation, and maintainable product functionality within the company environment.
