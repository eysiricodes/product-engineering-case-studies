# Edvolve Foundation

## Organisation

Edvolve Foundation supports digital-skills learning and training initiatives in Nigeria.

## Role

Tech Lead.

## Responsibilities

- Supported technical planning for digital training activities.
- Helped translate technical topics into practical learning experiences.
- Guided participants through product-building and implementation questions.
- Contributed technical perspective to training delivery, learning resources, and community support.

## Value Delivered

The role combines product engineering experience with practical teaching: helping learners move from abstract ideas to clearer technical decisions and usable digital outcomes.
