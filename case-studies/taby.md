# TABY

## Product

TABY is a privacy-first wellness app designed to help women, men, and couples understand patterns across reproductive health, mood, habits, vitality, relationships, and daily wellbeing. It brings related tracking experiences into one mobile product instead of leaving users to manage several disconnected tools.

Public links: [Website](https://officialtabyapp.com) | [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.taby.taby_app)

## Role

Founder and Full Stack Developer.

## Responsibilities

- Defined product direction, user flows, feature priorities, and release scope.
- Built and maintained the mobile product across its frontend, backend integrations, authentication, and data flows.
- Developed structured experiences for Female, Male, Couples, Mental Health, and Discipline modes.
- Implemented product integrations, in-app purchase logic, notifications, feedback/support flows, and app-store release work.
- Collected tester feedback, translated it into focused product changes, and managed post-launch iteration.

## Engineering Considerations

Because TABY handles sensitive wellness context, its product design needs clarity, consent-aware interaction, and restrained data handling. The engineering work prioritises understandable onboarding, reliable tracking flows, practical support paths, and clear separation between wellness information and professional medical care.

## Outcome

TABY progressed from an early product concept to a live Android application with freemium and premium access, early user feedback, and an active roadmap for reliability, growth, and broader distribution.
